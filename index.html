What it can do

Six radios and sensors on one 108 × 52 mm board.

📡

Sub-GHz radio

CC1101 transceiver (300–928 MHz) with an SMA jack, so you can screw on any antenna.

💳

NFC / RFID

PN532 reader for 13.56 MHz cards and tags. Read and explore your own cards.

🔴

Infrared

IR LED plus a VS1838B receiver. Learn and replay TV and AC remote codes.

📶

Wi-Fi + Bluetooth

ESP32 dual-core chip with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and BLE tools built into Bruce.

🖥️

2.0" color screen

240 × 320 ST7789 TFT, plus a 5-way D-pad and a BACK button.

🧪

Open & hackable

Bruce is open source, and so is the pin map. Flash new firmware over USB any time.

FAQ

Good to know before you buy.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. It comes flashed with Bruce, so you plug it in over USB-C and it's ready to use. You can flash updates later from the Bruce web flasher.

Does it come with a battery?

No. It runs from USB-C power, so a phone power bank works great.

How long does shipping take?

Each unit is built by hand. Please allow time for it to be assembled and tested before it ships. You'll get a message when yours is on the way.

Is this legal to use?

Yes, for learning and testing on things you own or have permission to test. Don't use it to transmit on frequencies you aren't allowed to use, to jam signals, or to access other people's devices, cards or networks. Follow your local laws (in the US, the FCC rules). By buying, you agree to use it responsibly.

Can I return it?

If it arrives broken or doesn't work, contact us and we'll fix or replace it.
