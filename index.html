What it's built to do

Radio, NFC, infrared, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on one 116 × 54 mm board.

Infrared

A built-in library of TV, projector and smart-board codes. Learn a button from your own remote, save it and send it again.

Sub-GHz radio

The CC1101 module plugs straight into the board, with its own screw-on antenna off the top edge. Signal meter, frequency analyzer, record and replay.

NFC

PN532 reader for 13.56 MHz cards and tags. There is no copper behind its antenna, which helps reading range.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

Scan nearby 2.4 GHz networks and Bluetooth LE devices and see their details.

Control it from your phone

Join the handheld's own Wi-Fi and open a page to see its screen live and press its buttons. No app needed, and it works on iPhone.

2.0" color screen

240 × 320 color screen facing out, with a D-pad, OK and BACK buttons.

Zaku OS

Our own firmware, written from scratch. Dark violet glass menus and six accent colors.

FAQ

Good to know.

When can I buy one?

Not yet. The rev C board still has to be made, built and tested. The price is planned at $50 plus shipping.

What changed in rev C?

The screen is turned the right way out, the CC1101 plugs straight in with no wires, the NFC area has no copper behind it, and there is a header for adding battery power.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Zaku OS will already be installed. Updates are flashed over USB-C.

Does it come with a battery?

No. It runs from USB-C, so a phone power bank works. The power header is for adding your own battery, charger and 5 V booster.

Is there a case?

Not yet. The earlier case doesn't fit rev C, so a new one comes after the board is tested.

Is this legal to use?

Only use it on things you own or have permission to test. Don't jam signals, transmit on frequencies you aren't allowed to use, or access other people's devices, cards or networks. Radio rules are different in each country, so check yours (in the US, the FCC rules).

What if it arrives broken?

Contact us and we'll fix or replace it.
